# 🧪 Pixelary Labs

**[Pixelary Labs](labs.thepixelary.com) is the Research & Development Division of [The Pixelary](https://thepixelary.com).**

**We explore the intersection of 3D graphics, generative AI, and LLM based automation.**

---

## 🌐 Overview

The Pixelary Labs serves as the experimental R&D and open-source engineering hub for The Pixelary, a Vancouver-based computer animation and visual technology studio founded by Mike Pan.

With over two decades of combined expertise across 3D computer graphics, software engineering, and visual media, Mike sits at the nexus of artistic craftsmanship and cutting-edge computation. The Labs department focuses on prototyping next-generation rendering workflows, machine learning techniques, and automation tools.
